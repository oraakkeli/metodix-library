# How do I answer a grouping question?

Some theses aren't rated on a scale — instead you **sort items into categories** (bins). The facilitator has given a few categories — for example *Hot · Warm · Cold* — and a list of items for you to place into them from your own point of view.

## How to place items

- **Drag** an item from one bin to another with your finger or mouse, **or**
- **Tap** an item first (it highlights), then tap the category you want it in.
- Remove an item from a bin with the **× button** next to it.

Both ways work — use whichever you prefer.

## One category or several?

- **One category:** each item belongs to exactly one category.
- **Several categories:** the same item may belong to more than one, if it fits.

The view tells you which rule applies. Usually **every item must be placed** before your answer is complete.

## Rationale and editing

As with other theses, you can add a **short rationale**: why you sorted things this way, and which items were borderline. The rationale is the most valuable part of a panel.

You can go back and adjust your sort before the round closes, and in the next round you'll see how others placed the items — and reconsider yours if you wish.
