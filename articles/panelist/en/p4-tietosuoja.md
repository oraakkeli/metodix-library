# Privacy — what happens to your data

A short note on what is collected about you and how it is handled, so you can take part
with full awareness.

## What is stored about you

Your responses (ratings and rationales), your comments, and possibly a name/email the
organizer added for the invitation. Other panelists see you **only by a pseudonym**
(e.g. P-03) — not your name.

The material is held on a server located in the EU, in accordance with EU data
protection law.

## Anonymity

The peer view is pseudonymous. Still note: if you write identifying details about yourself
or others in a rationale or comment, they can be revealed through the content. If anonymity
matters to you, avoid identifying details in free text.

## Is AI used?

The study may use AI (e.g. to group arguments or provide help). In that case your
**pseudonym (P-NN) and the content you write** may be sent for processing to Anthropic's
AI service — **your name and email are never sent**, and the material is not used to
train AI models. If the panel includes AI panelists, you will see a notice on the home
view before responding (see the article *AI panelists*).

## What happens when the study ends?

When the study is over, the organizer **anonymizes** it: your name, email and response
link are permanently deleted. The remaining material is anonymous research data that can
no longer be linked to you.

## Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, or **have it deleted**. Contact the study
organizer; deletion is technically supported. The full privacy & AI notice is publicly
available at **/privacy** (also linked in your invitation and on the home view).
