# How do I answer an allocation question?

Some theses ask you to **distribute a fixed total** (e.g. 100 points) across the options. This lets you show not just what matters most, but **how much** you weight each one.

## How to answer

- Enter a number for each option. **The sum must equal the given total** (e.g. 100) — you'll see the running sum and how much is left to distribute.
- You can give an option **zero** if you think it deserves no weight.
- Put points where you see the greatest significance; spread the rest across the less important options.

Your answer is complete only when the sum matches.

## Rationale

The most valuable part is a **short rationale**: where you concentrated points and what you left thin — and why. Rationales help others understand what's behind your weighting.

## Rounds

In the next round you'll see the panel's **average distribution** — where weight collectively concentrates and where estimates spread. You may reconsider your own allocation.
