# AI panelists — what they are and how to recognise them

In some studies the panel includes, alongside humans, **AI panelists**: AI-generated synthetic perspectives that respond to theses and take part in the dialogue. If your study includes AI panelists, you will always see a notice about it on the home view before responding.

## Why are AI panelists used?

The quality of a Delphi study depends on the diversity of perspectives. With AI panelists the facilitator can complement the panel with viewpoints missing from the human participants — for example a rare stakeholder or a marginal position. They do not replace humans; they enrich the conversation.

## How do you recognise an AI panelist?

AI-generated content is **always marked with the 🤖 indicator**, and AI panelists' codes start with an AI prefix. You never need to guess: if there is no marking, the author is human.

## Does this affect you?

Respond as you normally would — your own view and reasoning are the most valuable content of the study. You are welcome to challenge and comment on AI panelists' arguments just like anyone else's: the argument carries the weight, not its author. In the analysis phase, AI-generated content is kept separate from human responses, so your voice is never mixed with the machine's.

## What about data protection?

Your open responses may be processed by AI features (e.g. summaries). This always happens without your name and contact details — under a pseudonymous code only. See the data protection article for more.
