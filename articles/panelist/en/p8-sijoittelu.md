# How do I answer a 2×2 positioning question?

Some theses ask you to **place a point on a plane** with two axes — for example *impact* (horizontal) and *uncertainty* (vertical). The point shows where you position the thesis on both dimensions at once.

## How to answer

- **Tap or drag** to the spot you want inside the square. The point stays where you leave it.
- The axis names and ends are shown around the square: right/up = high, left/down = low.
- You can move the point freely until you're satisfied.

There's no right answer — it's your judgment on both axes.

## Rationale

The most valuable part is a **short rationale**: why that spot? Justify both dimensions — why this high/low on the horizontal axis, and why on the vertical. Rationales help others understand your positioning.

## Rounds

In the next round you'll see the panel's **positioning as a scatter plot** — where points cluster and where the panel's centroid sits. If the panel splits clearly into different corners, that signals real disagreement. You may reconsider your own position.
