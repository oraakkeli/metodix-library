# Welcome to Pronoia

Pronoia takes its name from ancient Greece: Pronoia means foresight — seeing ahead, far-sightedness. At Delphi, on the slopes of Mount Parnassus northwest of Athens, the road to the temple of prophecy passed the sanctuary of Athena Pronaia. Already in antiquity it was understood that not all answers come ready-made. They are born through asking, discussion and interpretation. Pronoia continues this tradition: the future is not predicted here — it is reasoned out together. You have been invited to the panel because your views and your reasons are part of that reasoning.

At the gate you are received by the AI assistant Kastalia. She is named after the spring — the Castalian spring — that rose by the entrance to the sanctuary at Delphi. Every traveller paused at the spring before stepping inside. Kastalia talks with you before the actual round: she opens up the questions and, if you wish, helps you put your thoughts into words and sharpen your reasons. There are two things she will never do: Kastalia does not judge your views, and she does not claim anything on your behalf. The voice is yours alone, and in a Delphi panel it is heard anonymously, so your argument carries by the force of its content, not its speaker.

When the rounds are over, your answers do not vanish into a summary table. Deeper "inside the temple" works a second assistant, Kassotis, named after another spring, whose water flowed into the innermost chamber of the temple. Kassotis helps the researcher see the structures and tensions in the panel's arguments, but interpretations and conclusions are always made by a human. AI carries the water; seeing is human work. The researcher makes sure that the understanding created by the panel also returns to the participants — it does not stay inside the temple.

Two words were carved into the temple wall at Delphi: Know thyself. That includes the limits of your own views — and that is exactly why the panel has been built: so that a group of knowledgeable people, unknown to one another, can together see further than anyone alone. At Delphi the god gave the signs, but it was the human who created the meaning. Welcome to the spring — the journey to the temple of the future begins here!
