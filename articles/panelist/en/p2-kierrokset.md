# Rounds, dialogue, and changing your answer

This is not an ordinary survey. The same questions are addressed over several rounds, and your view is allowed to develop along the way.

## What happens between rounds

When a round closes, you see a **group summary** — how the ratings were distributed — and the other panelists' **anonymized reasoning**. You don't see who said what, only the thoughts.

After that there may be a **dialogue** or a new round, where you can comment, respond to others' reasoning, and if you wish **refine your own answer**.

## May I change my mind?

Yes — and that is the whole point of the method. If someone's reasoning makes you reconsider, you can change your rating and briefly say why. This is not wavering but reflection. Equally, you may keep your position if the reasons don't change it — say why then too.

## The pause between rounds

Sometimes the study is briefly **paused** while the next round's questions are prepared from the previous results. You'll be notified when the next round opens. In the meantime you can review the previous round's material.
